About Biomassia

Our journey began with a simple mission:

To produce quality timber while preserving the forests it comes from. Since 2019 we have built an integrated site in Gua Musang where sawmilling, kiln drying and pelletising run under one roof — so offcuts and sawdust become product rather than waste.

  • Produce premium quality timber
  • Source responsibly
  • Support local communities
  • Innovate with sustainable practices
