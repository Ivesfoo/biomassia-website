Our Story

From one mill in Gua Musang

Biomassia Sdn. Bhd. was founded in 2019 in Gua Musang, Kelantan, to mill Malaysian timber properly and supply it to buyers who need it to be right every time.

What started as a sawmill became an integrated site. Plywood, sawn timber and wood pellets are now produced on the same ground, so the offcuts from one process feed the next instead of leaving as waste.

Today we supply industrial, commercial and export markets — milling to spec, drying to a consistent moisture content, and loading containers from our own yard.
